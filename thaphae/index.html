The role

What I did here

  • Ran the bar's marketing end to end: social media, event promotion, print and in-venue design, with sales and foot traffic as the scoreboard.
  • Helped manage Thai Fest, the bar's festival presence in downtown Toronto, from the first materials list to tear-down.
  • Designed brochures, posters and menus for a run of events: trivia nights, karaoke, happy hour and seasonal promotions.
  • Designed the logo for Rod Daeng, the karaoke bar inside the venue.
  • Shot and edited announcement videos, including the launch of the new pool-table section.
  • Started as Host, which is where the guest insight behind every promotion came from.

Event

Thai Fest

A downtown Toronto street festival where the bar served its menu to thousands of passers-by. The goal was simple: raise brand awareness and bring the diversity of Thai cuisine to a Canadian crowd that mostly knows pad thai.

From the materials list to the last plate

  • Planning: built the event materials list and timeline with the owners, then sourced everything from banners and menu boards to serving supplies and signage.
  • Brand presence: designed the booth graphics, menu cards and giveaways so the stall read as Thai Tavern from across the square.
  • On the day: worked the booth in traditional dress, hosted guests, ran content capture and kept the team on the plan.
  • After: turned the photos and clips into a week of social content and a follow-up push to bring festival visitors into the bar.

Design work

Print and digital pieces made to put people in seats: event promotion that lifted traffic on quiet nights, and a brand mark for the karaoke room.

Event promotion · Poster & social

Event brochures & posters

One of a series of event designs for the bar. This Trivia Night poster, co-hosted with Question Everything, ran as a print flyer, an Instagram post and a story, with a QR code for reservations.

  • Series covered trivia, karaoke, happy hour and seasonal promotions.
  • Each piece built around one job: fill a specific night and lift sales on it.
  • Designed in Canva to the bar's playful, colourful tone.
Promotion · Animated story

Sales promotions

Animated promo for a DoorDash buy-one-get-one on Pad Thai Sen Lek, November 1 to 14. Built as a vertical story so it ran on Instagram, in the bar and on the delivery listing with no re-cutting.

  • Part of a rolling calendar of offers designed to lift sales and traffic on slow weeks.
  • Same template reused across promotions, so each new offer took minutes, not days.
Brand identity · Logo

Rod Daeng Bar & Karaoke

Logo for the karaoke bar inside the venue. "Rod Daeng" is the red share-taxi truck of Chiang Mai, so the mark puts the truck front and centre in a vintage badge, with a palette that works on signage, menus and screens.

  • Badge format so it holds up as a door sign, a menu stamp and a social avatar.
  • Two-colour build for cheap, consistent printing.

Launch video

Announcement · Instagram Reel

The new pool-table section

When the bar opened a new pool-table section, I shot and edited the announcement video to get regulars back in and give new guests another reason to stay for one more round.

  • Planned the shots around the room's best angles and the busiest-looking moments.
  • Cut to a quick, hook-first rhythm with on-screen text so it reads with the sound off.
  • Posted alongside a promotion to drive traffic on the launch week.
