The role

What I do here

  • Own the content calendar for Milestone Energy across LinkedIn, Instagram and the website, from idea to published post.
  • Shoot on site. I go where the work is, rooftops included, to capture projects as they happen and turn them into short-form video.
  • Edit and caption every video: pacing, on-screen text, subtitles and the hook that earns the first three seconds.
  • Write the copy for posts, announcements and brochures in a voice that property managers actually read.
  • Design print and digital collateral: brochures, banners, booth graphics and social templates.
  • Support the group: the same work for sister companies MAXPower Services and CORE Plumbing & Mechanical, keeping three brands consistent.
  • Track what performs and feed it back into the next month's plan.
