Case study · Solis Movement × Be.infinit

Content Creator

2025 Toronto, ON Campaign content

Solis Movement is a Toronto wellness studio group: infrared hot pilates and yoga, reformer and strength classes across King West, Etobicoke and East Toronto, with 22.6K followers on Instagram. This campaign was a collaboration with the skincare brand Be.infinit.

@solis.movement ↗ @be.infinit ↗

Objective

Create compelling campaign content for Solis Movement × Be.infinit, connecting wellness, movement and community through engaging digital storytelling.

Responsibilities
  • Created visual content showcasing the wellness studio experience and the campaign collaboration.
  • Captured the energy and atmosphere of wellness activities through lifestyle-focused content.
  • Developed engaging content that aligned with the campaign's messaging and both brands' identities.
  • Highlighted the connection between fitness, well-being and community to engage the target audience.
